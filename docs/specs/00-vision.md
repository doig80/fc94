# 00 — Vision

**Status:** Draft — awaiting review

## Pitch

The arcade football game that made you fall in love with the sport in 1994, rebuilt for 2026.
Pick up a controller (or a keyboard, or your phone) and be scoring within thirty seconds.
Cartoon low-poly players under real floodlights, rain on the pitch, the crowd roaring —
but the controls are still three buttons.

## Inspiration

_FIFA International Soccer_ (1993, Mega Drive / SNES / PC): the diagonal "isometric"
camera, three-button controls, fast end-to-end play, bicycle kicks, the cheeky ref,
and above all, playing it on one sofa with friends. We take the **feel**, not the name, teams or assets.

## Pillars

1. **Instant fun.** Three action buttons, sensible defaults, no tutorial needed to score.
   A first-time player should enjoy their first match against the easiest CPU.
2. **Easy to learn, room to master.** Depth comes from timing and positioning
   (one-twos, through balls, curled shots), not from button combos.
3. **Short, punchy matches.** Default 2 × 3 minutes. Quick restarts. No loading walls.
4. **Looks great anywhere.** Cartoon low-poly style with modern lighting, materials and weather,
   scaling from a phone browser to a gaming PC.
5. **Better together.** Local multiplayer (2–4 players, same screen) is a first-class mode.

## Audience

- Adults who played 90s football games and want that feeling back.
- Casual players and kids who find modern football sims overwhelming.
- Friends and families looking for a couch game that runs in a browser with no install.

## v1 scope (headline)

- Friendly match and a small tournament (e.g. fictional "world cup") mode.
- 1P vs CPU, and 2–4 local players with keyboard/gamepads. Touch: 1P vs CPU.
- Fictional national teams.
- Weather (clear, rain, snow) and time of day (day, dusk, night under floodlights).
- Accessibility basics: remappable controls, assist options, colour-blind-safe kits, readable UI.

## Non-goals for v1

- No real leagues, clubs, players, or likenesses (see [03](03-legal-and-ip.md)).
- No career / manager mode, transfers, or ultimate-team style collecting.
- No online multiplayer (planned for v2 — architecture prepared, see [01](01-platform-and-tech.md)).
- No monetisation, accounts, or ads.
- No simulation-grade realism in gameplay; realism is for the _look_, arcade is for the _play_.

## Success criteria

- A new player scores within their first match without reading instructions.
- 60 fps on a mid-range laptop; playable (≥ 30 fps) on a 3-year-old phone.
- Loads to the main menu in under 10 seconds on a typical broadband connection.
- Friends ask to play "one more match".
