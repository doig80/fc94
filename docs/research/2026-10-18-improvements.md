# Improvement research: mechanics and graphics (2026-10-18)

**Purpose:** ideas that could make the game more fun or better looking, with evidence, cost and a suggested
milestone. **Nothing here is in scope yet.** You decide what to include and where it fits, in [#19](https://github.com/doig80/fc94/issues/19).

**Sizes:** S = under a day · M = a few days · L = a week or more.

## Mechanics

| ID      | Proposal                                                                                                                                                                                  | Why (evidence)                                                                                                                                                                                | Size | Suggested milestone                                                                       | My view                                           |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **M1**  | **Aftertouch:** for a split second after a shot or pass, the stick still bends it (swerve) or lifts and drops it                                                                          | Sensible Soccer's signature: "a huge amount of control" from one button, and "a subtle art to be mastered". It fits our "easy to learn, room to master" pillar. Builds on our curl-at-release | S    | Vertical slice (as a tuning switch, to compare)                                           | **Strongly recommend trying it**                  |
| **M2**  | **Looser dribbling option:** the ball is pushed ahead in touches (Kick Off) instead of staying at the feet                                                                                | Kick Off's ball "doesn't stick to your foot", which made tackling "a matter of skill rather than of chance". It's harder to learn, though                                                     | M    | Vertical slice playtest (tuning slider), then decide per difficulty (e.g. looser on Hard) | Try as a slider; keep sticky as default           |
| **M3**  | **Game feel ("juice"):** a 50–100 ms freeze on power shots and goals, a small camera kick, net ripple, ball trail on hard shots, short slow-motion on the winning goal, controller rumble | "Juice it or lose it" (GDC 2012) showed how much this adds; research ties impact feel to hit-stop, sound and camera. Rumble works in Chromium browsers (feature-detect elsewhere)             | S–M  | Vertical slice (with #15)                                                                 | **Recommend**: cheap and makes scoring feel great |
| **M4**  | **Perfect-timing reward:** first-time shots and volleys struck in a tight timing window get extra power or accuracy and a visual flash                                                    | Mario Strikers' "Perfect" pass-and-shot rewards timing without extra buttons                                                                                                                  | S    | Full match                                                                                | Recommend after AI exists                         |
| **M5**  | **Exploit-proof keepers:** keeper AI with distinct save types (catch, parry, tip over), plus automated "exploit bots" that try known cheese                                               | FIFA '94 had a notorious exploit (stand in front of the keeper so the ball rebounds in). We can test for this automatically                                                                   | M    | #14 (AI) and Full match                                                                   | Recommend (part of #14)                           |
| **M6**  | **Input-latency target:** button to kick ≤ 50 ms, measured in a test                                                                                                                      | FIFA '94's main criticism was input lag; a modern arcade football game failed on "sluggish controls". We already buffer presses                                                               | S    | Vertical slice                                                                            | Recommend: add to spec 10 §8                      |
| **M7**  | **Set-piece aiming:** free kicks and corners aimed with a target marker (FIFA '94 used a box)                                                                                             | Makes set pieces readable and skilful                                                                                                                                                         | S–M  | #13 / Full match                                                                          | Recommend                                         |
| **M8**  | **Automatic balance testing:** thousands of CPU-vs-CPU matches per CI run, checking feel targets (3–6 goals per match, pass times, possession balance)                                    | Our deterministic simulation runs headless and fast, a rare advantage. It catches balance regressions without playtests                                                                       | M    | Once the AI exists (#14)                                                                  | **Strongly recommend**                            |
| **M9**  | **Share a goal:** a link that replays your goal (deterministic replay = just the inputs)                                                                                                  | Social and viral; cheap thanks to determinism                                                                                                                                                 | M    | v1.1                                                                                      | Nice to have                                      |
| **M10** | **Practice challenges:** short skill games (curl into a corner, one-two then score) as the tutorial                                                                                       | Teaches the depth (one-twos, curl) the vision promises, in a fun way                                                                                                                          | M    | Alpha (Practice mode)                                                                     | Recommend                                         |

## Graphics

| ID     | Proposal                                                                                                                                                                              | Why (evidence)                                                                                                                                                                 | Size                | Suggested milestone                                           | My view                                  |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| **G1** | **Animated crowd** using baked vertex-animation textures on our instanced crowd (cheering on goals, Mexican wave)                                                                     | Babylon supports baked animation textures on thin instances: thousands of animated fans for almost the cost of static ones. Spec 01 §5 already wants an animated crowd on High | M                   | Beta                                                          | Recommend                                |
| **G2** | **Real floodlight pools** with clustered lighting (Forward+), many lamps per tower                                                                                                    | Babylon 9's clustered lights work on WebGPU **and** WebGL2 and make many lights cheap. It's the missing night-match look (and fixes the toon limitation)                       | S–M                 | Full match or Beta                                            | **Recommend**: big win for night matches |
| **G3** | **Volumetric floodlight beams** in rain or mist at night                                                                                                                              | Babylon's frame-graph volumetric lighting; heavier, WebGPU-first                                                                                                               | M                   | Beta, Ultra only                                              | Optional                                 |
| **G4** | **TAA** (clean edges on thin pitch lines and nets) and **screen-space reflections** on a wet pitch                                                                                    | Both in Babylon; spec 01 §5 already lists SSR for Ultra                                                                                                                        | S each              | Beta                                                          | Recommend TAA; SSR for Ultra             |
| **G5** | **Better grass:** detailed texture with hex tiling (no visible repeats, Babylon 9.7+), subtle normal map, worn goalmouths during a match, snow settling                               | Hex tiling is built in; the pitch is the largest thing on screen                                                                                                               | S–M                 | Beta                                                          | Recommend                                |
| **G6** | **Start the character pipeline early:** one real low-poly player model (CC0) with Mixamo animations retargeted by Babylon's AnimatorAvatar, blended run/sprint, a procedural kick aim | Spec 01 §8 lists animation as the top quality risk. Babylon 9 has retargeting and animation blending built in, so trying it early de-risks Beta                                | M (test) / L (full) | A **character test** during Full match; full roll-out in Beta | **Recommend** the early test             |
| **G7** | **Readability aids:** ball blob shadow always visible, a subtle trail on fast balls, an off-screen indicator for your player                                                          | Arcade games live on readability; cheap                                                                                                                                        | S                   | Vertical slice                                                | Recommend                                |
| **G8** | **Kit colours:** fix red reading pink (exposure or tone-mapping tweak), add an automatic contrast check between kits                                                                  | Found in this review (N9); also needed for colour-blind-safe kits                                                                                                              | S                   | Art round (spec 20) / Alpha                                   | Recommend                                |

## Suggested packaging (if you agree with my views)

- **Vertical slice, after #13–#16:** M1 (aftertouch switch), M3 (juice), M6 (latency target), G7 (readability).
- **Full match:** M4, M5, M7, G2 (floodlights), G6 (character test).
- **Alpha:** M8 (balance bots, once the AI exists), M10 (practice challenges).
- **Beta:** G1, G3 (optional), G4, G5, G8.
- **v1.1:** M9 (share a goal).
